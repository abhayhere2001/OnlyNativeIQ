OnlyNative Shipping and Delivery Policy
Document ID: ON-POL-0001
Version: 2.0
Status: Approved
Access Level: Public

Current delivery area: Bengaluru.
Delivery is free within 2 km of Horamavu, Bengaluru.
Beyond 2 km, Porter delivery charges apply. OnlyNativeIQ must not invent or promise a fixed Porter charge.
Pune and Hyderabad are planned expansion locations and are not currently serviced.
Orders are made to order. Preparation begins after receipt of the order and orders are normally dispatched the next day.
International shipping is not currently available.
