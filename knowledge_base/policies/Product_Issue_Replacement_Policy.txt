OnlyNative Product Issue and Replacement Policy
Document ID: ON-POL-0003
Version: 2.0
Status: Approved
Access Level: Public

If a customer reports a genuine product-related issue, OnlyNative will review the concern and take appropriate action.
Where appropriate, a fresh replacement product will be provided.
A monetary cash/UPI refund is not promised by the current approved policy.
No mandatory complaint time window, photo requirement or refund-processing timeline has been formally defined in this corpus.
