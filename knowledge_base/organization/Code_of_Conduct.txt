OnlyNative Code of Conduct
Document ID: ON-HR-0002
Version: 2.0
Status: Approved
Access Level: Internal - Employee Only

Employees must behave respectfully with colleagues, supervisors, customers and delivery partners.
Smoking and alcohol are prohibited in the kitchen and workplace.
OnlyNative recipes and formulations are confidential. Employees must not photograph, copy, share or disclose them outside the organization.
Employees must wash hands before food handling and after breaks and use hand wash and sanitizer.
Head caps, gloves and aprons are required while handling food.
Employees who are sick or have an infectious condition must avoid food preparation and inform the supervisor or owner.
Employees must clean their work area, utensils and equipment after production.
Unauthorized removal or misuse of ingredients, finished products, equipment or other company property is prohibited.
Production and work areas may be monitored using CCTV/security cameras.
Mobile phone use is prohibited during working hours except emergency use with supervisor permission.
Disciplinary action may include verbal or written warning, corrective action, and termination for serious or repeated misconduct.
