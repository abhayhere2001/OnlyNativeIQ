OnlyNative was founded in 2025 by Anita Kumari and is based in Bengaluru, Karnataka, India.

OnlyNative was created around a simple belief: traditional food is not merely something we eat. It is connected to family, festivals, memories and the places we call home. The aim is to bring authentic traditional snacks and native recipes to modern homes while staying faithful to the flavours and values behind them.

Authenticity begins with honest ingredients, traditional methods and transparency about what goes into the food. OnlyNative procures ingredients from reliable sources, prepares products in small batches and focuses on freshly prepared products.

Founder Story
Anita Kumari is a homemaker and cooking enthusiast who hails from Bihar. She learned traditional recipes from her mother and grandmother, with knowledge passed through generations. Her cooking knowledge includes products such as Thekua, Gujiya, Nimki and other traditional foods.

Culinary Focus
The current culinary focus is primarily traditional foods associated with Bihar and Uttar Pradesh. The long-term product philosophy is broader: OnlyNative intends to identify and make available authentic native foods from different parts of India, including regional traditions from states such as Karnataka, Jharkhand and others.

Official Tagline
Apni Mitti, Apni Virasat.

Market and Expansion
OnlyNative currently serves Bengaluru. Near-term expansion is planned for Pune and Hyderabad. The long-term vision is to make OnlyNative products available across India and internationally, including the Americas, Europe, the Middle East and Australia.

Mission
To take authentic native products across India and, over time, to customers in other countries.

Meaning of the Name
No separate literal definition of the name OnlyNative has been formally provided. In the approved brand context, it represents the focus on authentic native recipes, traditions, ingredients and connection to one's roots.
